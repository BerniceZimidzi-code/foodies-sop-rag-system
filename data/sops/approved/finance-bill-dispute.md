# Customer Bill Dispute and Refund Handling

## 1. Dispute intake

Cashiers must listen to the customer concern, review the transaction details, and check the receipt before escalating. If the bill is incorrect, the cashier must first verify the itemised order against the till record.

## 2. Refund approval

Refunds must be approved by a supervisor or finance lead before payment is returned. Cashiers must not issue cash refunds outside approved procedures.

## 3. Documenting the resolution

All bill disputes and refunds must be recorded in the daily dispute register with the customer name, amount, reason, resolution, and approving authority.
