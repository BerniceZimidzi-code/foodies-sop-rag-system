# Food Storage and Cold Chain Monitoring

## 1. Cold room checks

Kitchen staff must check walk-in refrigerator and cold room temperatures at the start of each shift and every two hours thereafter. Record the temperature in the kitchen temperature log and report any reading above 5°C immediately to the Head Chef.

## 2. Corrective action

If a refrigerator temperature is above the approved range, move the affected food items to a backup cold storage unit if available. If backup storage is unavailable, inform the Head Chef and Food Safety Manager before serving any product from that unit.

## 3. Food receiving

All perishable ingredients must be checked on receipt. Products that are not cold when received must be rejected or recorded for supervisor review before use.
