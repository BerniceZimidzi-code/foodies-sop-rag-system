# Guest Complaint Handling and Service Recovery

## 1. Standard complaint response

When a guest raises a complaint, the staff member must listen without interruption, acknowledge the concern, and apologize for the experience. The staff member should record the complaint in the service log immediately.

## 2. Escalation process

If the issue cannot be resolved immediately, the staff member must notify the floor supervisor or shift manager without delay. The supervisor is responsible for reviewing the complaint and confirming the corrective action.

## 3. Recovery options

Approved recovery options include a replacement meal, a complimentary beverage, or an agreed discount approved by management. Staff must not offer compensation beyond the approved recovery list without supervisor approval.
